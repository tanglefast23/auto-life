## NOT VERIFIED HERE — continuous frame rate

**`requestAnimationFrame` fires 0 frames in 6.2 seconds in a headless pane** (measured with an in-page probe). The page is never composited, so RAF is suspended and the app — correctly, per SPEC §5/C1 — stays hard-paused. Overriding `document.visibilityState` does not help; RAF throttling follows the compositor, not the JS-visible property. The single frame that did fire arrived with a large delta and was capped at exactly 8 ticks, which is `MAX_TICKS_PER_FRAME` doing its job.

So the master §5 exit's frame evidence cannot come from this environment. Two things were done instead, and neither is a substitute for a human watching a real browser:

1. **`watched-day.test.ts`** covers everything that does not need a compositor: a full 1440-tick day, byte-identical frames at 1×/2×/4×, the sim never off the walkable grid, the character quad never outside the room, sub-tile interpolation actually occurring, and the §7.1 routine genuinely present (`toilet`, `brush`, `shower`, `meal`, `sleep` all appear).
2. **Real-browser fps is carried to the Vercel deployment**, where the on-screen readout reports scale, DPR, exactness, fps, and tick count off the running build.

Precedent for carrying a deferral this way: P0 recorded browser close/reopen as a NOT VERIFIED deferral rather than claiming it.

## Defects found and fixed during P3

**The world rendered nothing until tick 1.** A game opened paused — or simply the first frame — was blank, even though the tilemap, objects, and start position are all known at t=0. `buildSnapshot()` was extracted from `step()` so the loop seeds a snapshot in its constructor. Regression-tested (`the first frame already has a world`).

**The scale solver reported valid steps as inexact.** It iterated fractional scales by subtracting `1/dpr`, accumulating float error: at DPR 3 it produced 5.666667, whose product with 3 is 17.000000000000004. Rewritten to iterate **integer physical-pixels-per-art-pixel**, making exactness structural rather than an epsilon comparison.

**A commit landed without a file it depended on.** `31ed468` staged `src/render` and `src/sim/step.ts` but not `src/sim/render-view.ts`, so that tree was missing the `pose` field `WorldScene` selects sprites from and would not have typechecked. Caught by reading `git status` after committing instead of trusting the add; fixed in `fde218e`. Working-tree green is not commit green.

## The watch findings (master §5: what a rendered day actually shows)

Measured on the current engine at ENGINE_VERSION 5, seed 1234, steady state:

- **79% of waking minutes are idle** (745 of 947). Expected at P3 and not a defect: Practice is player-only and there is no queue yet, so nothing fills the free hours. It is recorded because the number carries into P4, where the queue is what fills it.
- **Sleep is 493 game-minutes = 4.1 real minutes at 1×**, a third of a 12-minute day. §11.4's dissolve-to-morning is the answer and **is owned by no phase** — master §5 gives P4 "the first-night recap" and P5 "remaining recap", and the skip itself is in neither row. Flagged so it cannot vanish silently (master §3).
- **There is a one-tick idle gap between consecutive activities** (e.g. `07:08 idle → 07:09 brush`). At 1× that reads as a half-second freeze at every transition. Cosmetic, but it happens at every transition, so it will be visible.
- Of a 12-minute day at 1×, roughly **101 real seconds** has the sim doing anything. Travel is only ~32 game-minutes a day, so there is little screen time in which to observe the `m_speed` walk-tempo effect §11.3 promises.
